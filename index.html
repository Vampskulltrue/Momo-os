The idea

An OS that feels
like a handheld.

Not another Android skin overloaded with stuff. Momo OS is imagined as a focused layer between the hardware and the games you actually want to play.

⌁

Console-first UI

A controller-friendly interface with big targets, quick navigation and almost no reason to touch the screen.

◒

Game Library

One clean home for Android games, emulators, ports and your recently played titles.

☼

Sleep & Resume

Designed around the handheld habit: close it, put it down, pick it back up and keep playing.

⌘

Button-native

Every important action has a controller path, so the touch screen stays optional instead of mandatory.

◌

Momo Mode

Soft animations, tiny sounds and warm visual details give the OS its own personality without getting in the way.

⚙

Power tools

Performance profiles, brightness, audio, mappings and system shortcuts gathered in one quick panel.
